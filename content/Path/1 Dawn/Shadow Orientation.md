- **[[Bedrock]] - Internal Breakdown** _(The foundation cracks before anything else can be built.)_
    - **Entrenchment** → When **mind** hardens, growth stops. _(Dogma, rigidity, stagnation.)_
    - **Decay** → When **body** fails, vitality erodes. _(Neglect, deterioration, rot.)_
    - **Excess** → When **spirit** overflows, it distorts. _(Overindulgence, obsession, uncontrolled chaos.)_
- **[[Callings]] - Social & Institutional Corruption** _(Failure to engage wisely in the world.)_
    - **Fragmentation** → When **[[Networks]]** collapse, isolation follows. _(Tribalism, disconnection, distrust.)_
    - **Corruption** → When **[[State]]** loses its purpose, power consumes itself. _(Tyranny, dysfunction, rot from within.)_
    - **Despair** → When **[[God]]** is lost, meaning crumbles. _(Cynicism, nihilism, existential collapse.)_
- **[[Enchantments]] - Power Turned Destructive** _(Creation can become domination, stagnation, or ruin.)_
    - **Oppression** → When **Conflict** stops progress, it enslaves. _(Control, domination, violence for its own sake.)_
    - **Stagnation** → When **Culture** ceases to evolve, it ossifies. _(Decay of tradition, lifeless repetition.)_
    - **Degeneration** → When **Growth** runs unchecked, it implodes. _(Runaway consumption, unchecked chaos.)_
- **[[Legacies]] - Structural Failures Over Time** _(Even the greatest systems will crack if they are not built to last.)_
    - **Fracture** → When **Resolution** fails, order splinters. _(Unresolved tensions, fragmentation, lost direction.)_
    - **Discord** → When **Harmony** is broken, division reigns. _(Polarization, disharmony, cycles of collapse.)_
    - **Hubris** → When **Ascendancy** overreaches, it falls. _(Arrogance, unsustainable ambition, downfall.)_
- **[[Resonances]] - The Corruption of Meaning Itself** _(When the unseen forces guiding existence distort or disappear.)_
    - **Void** → When **Vetra (Structure & Logic)** unravels, meaninglessness takes hold. _(Entropy, the collapse of form.)_
    - **Distortion** → When **Sonara (Resonance & Transmission)** is twisted, truth is lost. _(Noise, misalignment, misinformation.)_
    - **Hollowing** → When **Eidoria (Expression & Form)** is drained, only the shell remains. _(Imitation, superficiality, empty aesthetics.)_
