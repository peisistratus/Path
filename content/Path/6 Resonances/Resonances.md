**Sections**
- **[[Orphics]]** ([[Ordos]], [[Sonara]], [[Eidoria]]) - The unseen currents of existence, weaving echoes into new forms. Some forces build, some dissolve, and some carry us beyond what we once thought possible. And their shadows (_Corrosion, Distortion, and Immanence_) When cycles turn inward, Renewal breeds Corrosion, Reverberation warps into Distortion, and Transcendence collapses into Immanence—what should propel us forward instead deteriorates, misleads, and holds us captive in what is.
- **[[Death]]** - Not an endpoint, but a passage. It is the dissolution of what was, making way for what will be.
- **[[New Eyes]]** - You have walked the path. Now, you see it anew. And just beyond, someone stands at its threshold, uncertain where it leads. What will you tell them?

_The real voyage of discovery consists not in seeing new sights, but in looking with new eyes.  
 - _Marcel Proust_ #quotes 

What happens after the ascent? The journey does not end, there is always a fresh horizon. There will always be new balances to find, new callings to answer, new enchantments to create, and new legacies to leave behind. The echoes of the path do not fade, they become the first step for those who follow.
